## 2025-05-14 - [Comprehensive Keyboard Accessibility & Focus Management]
**Learning:** In high-luxury photography portfolios with custom cursor effects (cursor: none), high-contrast :focus-visible outlines (2px solid #fff) are essential for keyboard navigation, as the default cursor is invisible. Negative outline-offsets help maintain aesthetic integrity for grid items.
**Action:** Always implement :focus-visible with sufficient contrast and programmatic focus management (tabindex="-1" + .focus()) for overlays to ensure a seamless screen reader experience.
