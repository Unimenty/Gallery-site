## 2026-06-20 - Focus Management in Dynamic & Custom Cursor Interfaces
**Learning:** High-fashion/editorial sites often use `cursor: none` and custom grid logic that breaks standard accessibility. In this repo, the infinite gallery items were unreachable because they were div-based and lacked tabindex. Furthermore, even if focus was applied, the custom cursor implementation (which hides the native cursor) meant focus rings were effectively invisible unless explicitly styled with high contrast `:focus-visible`.
**Action:** Always implement a global `FocusManager` (tracking `lastFocusedElement`) for sites with multiple overlay systems (Lightbox, Nav, Services). Ensure `:focus-visible` is styled with `outline-offset` to remain visible inside container boundaries and visible even when `cursor: none` is active on the body.
